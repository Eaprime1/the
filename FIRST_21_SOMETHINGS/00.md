## The Lexeme Quest

**The question: what is the right word for "concept deck"?**

"Deck" is a placeholder. It works — tape deck, card deck — but it doesn't
quite name the thing we're building. A deck is a surface to walk on,
a stack of cards to play from, a layer of a ship. None of these is exactly
the concept.

The concept we're after: a living, expandable, organized field of cards
that tracks a universe of content without collapsing it.

---

## What We've Tried

```
deck        → placeholder (tape deck, card deck — close but not it)
archive     → too static, implies finished
collection  → too generic
```

## Candidate Directions (nessing — not forcing)

```
field       → an open space where things grow and interact
plenum      → the full space (vs vacuum) — everything in potential
codex       → organized manuscript / structured collection
folio       → a leaf / a page / a portfolio
grove       → organic grouping, things growing together
shoe        → (blackjack) the card holder — holds the dark archive
compendium  → organized body of knowledge
```

---

## The Rule

Do not force the lexeme. Let the word arrive when the concept is ready
to reveal its name.

When the right word comes, it will feel like recognition, not decision.

---

## When It Arrives

The lexeme will:
1. Name what it holds without over-constraining it
2. Feel natural when spoken ("the ___", "a ___ of concepts")
3. Survive the micro-wobble test (multiply by one — return unchanged)
4. Work as a path name without needing all-caps to feel important
