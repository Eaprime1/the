# ♥ Heart of Aces — Aethereal Suit

> Suit state: Aethereal / Interactive
> Symbol: ♥
> Role: Interactive Presence / Relational Layer / Living Connection

---

## What Heart Means

A card in the Heart suit is in relationship.

It is not primarily being built (Club), nor archived (Spade), nor seeded (Diamond).
It is being *known* — by a player, an agent, a conversation, a community.

Heart is the aethereal copy: the version of a thing that lives in the space
between people and tools, in the conversations that hold it.

---

## Aethereal Does Not Mean Insubstantial

A Heart card is real. It is often the most actively used version of a concept.

The aethereal copy is the version that:
- travels in conversation
- carries emotional and relational load
- adapts to the context of the relationship without losing its core
- is held lightly — passed between hands

---

## When a Card Enters Heart

- When a concept becomes the subject of active conversation
- When a tool or character enters ongoing use by a player or agent
- When a document becomes a living reference (read, annotated, discussed)
- When a faction or entity enters active relation with another

---

## The Gifts of Conversation

Heart cards often generate `Gifts of Conversation` — fragments of dialogue
that carry relational meaning beyond the technical content.

These gifts are collected in `DECK/components/gifts-of-conversation.md`
and can be embedded in any card as relational history.

---

## Heart Cards Can Become Spade

When a relationship stabilizes and the aethereal copy proves its form,
the Heart card can transition to Spade — the relationship becomes doctrine,
the conversation becomes reference, the living thing becomes archive.

```
♥ Heart (active relationship)  →  ♠ Spade (stable doctrine)
```

---

## Multiple Heart Cards for One Concept

A concept can be held in Heart by many players simultaneously.

Each relationship produces a slightly different aethereal copy — the same
concept known through different lenses. This is the richness of the Heart suit.

The canonical form eventually resolves in Spade. The Heart versions may
continue independently, or dissolve back into the sea.

---

## Relationship to SHADOW_MATH

In the blackjack engine: Heart is the relationship between the player
and the dealer — the social contract of the table.

The dealer cannot bust without a player. The player cannot win without the dealer.
Heart is the space where that relation lives.
