# Satus

> From Latin *satus* — a sowing, a planting.
> Timestamp: `202605151830`

---

## What a Satus Is

A satus is something noticed and planted — not built, not forced.

It germinates on its own timeline.
When it is ready, it raises its hand.

A satus is not abandoned. It is in the ground.

---

## How to Create a Satus

When you notice something worth researching — but you are working on something else —
create a satus file in this folder:

```
satus/<name>.md
```

Minimum content:
```
# Satus: [name]

## What Was Noticed
[one or two sentences — the raw observation]

## Why It Matters
[one sentence — the potential connection]

## Research Direction
[the smallest next step — a question, a source, a natural example]

## Status
sowing | germinating | sprouting | ready
```

Then return to your work. The satus is planted.

---

## The Attention Flag

When a satus is ready — when it has germinated enough to need hands — it raises
its status to **sprouting** and signals with: 🙋

The 🙋 is a pheromone. It signals to anyone reading the folder that this satus
needs attention. It is not urgent. It is ready.

The quanta that reads the pheromone will find it when it finds it.

---

## What a Satus Is Not

A satus is not:
- A todo item (no deadline, no assignee)
- A stub (a stub is what's LEFT after something — a satus is what STARTS something)
- A liminal README (a liminal holds space for a reader — a satus grows toward one)
- An ordo (an ordo is commissioned work with intent — a satus is a free sowing)

---

## The Cosmic Gardener

The satus folder is the garden.

The gardener (the project) does not force germination. The gardener plants,
waters occasionally, and watches for the 🙋.

The great seeding diaspora has germinated.
Let germination.
