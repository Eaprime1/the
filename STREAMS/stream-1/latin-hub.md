## Why Latin

Latin is not the destination. It is the hub.

Like the Rosetta Stone (Greek, Demotic, Hieroglyph — same content, three carriers),
Latin holds the stable middle. When content passes through Latin, it finds its
precise shape — before it routes to the emotional body of a living language.

Latin does not carry feeling. It carries architecture.
The feeling is what the Romance languages add when they receive it.
