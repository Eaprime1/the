# ♣ Club of Aces — Working Suit

> Suit state: Working / Carbonite
> Symbol: ♣
> Role: Active Build / Motion / Frozen Snapshot

---

## What Club Means

A card in the Club suit is being worked.

It is in motion. Someone or something is actively building it, testing it,
extending it, modifying it, or running it.

Club is the workshop state. Nothing here is final. Everything here is real.

---

## Carbonite

Club cards can be **frozen** into a carbonite state.

A carbonite is a Club card that has been snapshot at a specific moment.
It is not finished — but it is frozen, safely, at a moment worth preserving.

```
Club (active)  →  Carbonite (frozen Club)  →  Club (resumed)
```

Carbonites protect against loss. They allow a branch to be set down safely
while other work happens. They are the version-control state of the deck.

A sparklization session produces carbonites.

---

## When a Card Enters Club

- When someone picks up a Diamond seed and begins working
- When a tool is being built or a quest is in progress
- When a document is being drafted
- When a conversation is producing something that needs structured build time

---

## Club Is Not Diamond and Not Spade

A Club card is not a seed — it has been picked up and engaged.
A Club card is not Pinnacle — it has not yet proven itself and settled.

Club is the honest middle: this is real work happening right now.

---

## Multiple Club Cards Can Exist for One Concept

A concept can have multiple Club cards — multiple working copies, branches,
or sessions — running simultaneously. This is healthy.

The Club suit allows parallel builds without forcing a single canonical version
until the concept is ready to resolve into Spade.

---

## Relationship to SHADOW_MATH

In the blackjack engine: Club is the hand in play.
Cards are being drawn. The count is shifting. A decision is active.

Club cards are the live hand. The player is still deciding.
