Postremum Onus — One More Mission

Somewhere between a federal land-use comment period and an appropriations bill, a small fellowship of repositories — the, prima, and radix — learned what it means to be finalized. This page is the souvenir from that trip: equal parts glossary, logbook, and reassurance that nothing here will bite. Mostly.

Glossary

Finalize Button
A comment — @claude finalize — that closes the review period on a PR, the way a sign-off closes a comment period on a federal land-use report. Checks that every review thread is resolved, posts a sealed summary, and stamps the PR finalized.
Rubric & Valuation
The scoring table used on every reviewed PR: Correctness, Consistency, Scope discipline, Verification — out of 5 each — plus an overall valuation (Low / Medium / High). Less paperwork, more "did this hold up."
Webhook Subscription
The thing that actually works for "keep an eye on this PR," as opposed to a cron job with no token and no clock. Comments, CI runs, and reviews arrive as events; no polling, no noise.
Postremum Onus
Latin for "the last burden" or "one final task" — the title chosen for this session's closing chapter. One more volley before the line stands down.
The BBS (Deferred)
A whole dial-up-flavored trade network — quantum_telegard, custom doors, a pinnacle node calling home with updates — sketched out, then set down on purpose. "Let's wait on the BBS" is its own kind of finalization: knowing what not to build yet.
Session Scope
Three repositories were in reach this round — the, prima, radix — out of forty-four non-forked repos under the same owner. The other forty-one are not lost, just not yet invited to the party.

When Is It Safe to Archive?

Not at the blue "open" dot. Not at draft. A conversation — like a PR — is safe to set down once the work is actually merged, ideally carrying the finalized label. Anything earlier is still a comment period, and comment periods don't get filed away.
