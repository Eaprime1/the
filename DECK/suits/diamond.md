# ♦ Diamond of Aces — Seed Suit

> Suit state: Seed
> Symbol: ♦
> Role: Origin / Foundational / Being Born

---

## What Diamond Means

A card in the Diamond suit is being seeded.

It has just arrived. It has not yet been worked. It has not yet been tested.
But it is real — it exists, it has a name, it has a lineage possibility.

Diamond is the moment of recognition before the motion begins.

---

## When a Card Enters Diamond

- When a concept arrives that has not yet been sorted
- When a fragment surfaces from the dark archive that deserves a handle
- When a new idea, entity, character, tool, or workflow is first named
- When a conversation produces something that needs to be held before it evaporates

Every card starts as Diamond or returns to Diamond when reseeding.

---

## The Seed Is Not Fragile

A Diamond card is not incomplete in a broken way.

It is complete as a seed. A seed holds the full potential of the tree
in a form small enough to survive the journey.

The Diamond card holds:
- the concept's name
- its first recognition
- its lineage (what it came from)
- its first question (what it is asking to become)

---

## Diamond Cards in the Intake Flow

The Card Intake protocol (see `CARD_INTAKE.md`) catches incoming concepts
before they are sorted. A card is treated as a Diamond until the intake
questions reveal its true current suit.

Most incoming material is Diamond until proven otherwise.

---

## Diamond → Club Transition

A Diamond card becomes Club when:
- someone picks it up and begins working with it
- it gets a file, a schema, a branch, or a session
- motion begins

The transition is marked by the first active use, not by a declaration.

---

## Relationship to Dark Nessing

Diamond is the first crystallization out of the dark nessing.

The dark nesses. Then — at some moment — a shape appears.
That shape is the Diamond card. It is the first thing that can be named.

```
dark nessing → ♦ Diamond (first crystallization)
```
