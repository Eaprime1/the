# Synergy Spark — Mission Deck

> Deck type: emergent / relational
> Role: cross-deck interaction / emergent connection / resonance

---

## Purpose

The Synergy Spark deck is the fourth deck — the emergent result of the
other three interacting.

```
Work Deck       ↘
Play Wonka      → Synergy Spark (emergent)
Create Pandora  ↗
```

It cannot be planned directly. It appears when the other decks are all active
and something unexpected connects them.

---

## Synergy Character

Synergy is not cooperation — it is emergence.

When a Work Deck task and a Play Wonka experiment and a Pandora opening
all happen in proximity, and something new appears that none of them
could have produced alone: that is Synergy Spark.

---

## What Belongs Here

```
- The moment when two unrelated cards recognize each other
- A metaphor from Play Wonka that solves a Work Deck problem
- A tool built for one context that works in three others
- The connections between AI tools in conversation about the same project
  (Claude + Gemini + ChatGPT + Perplexity + NotebookLM all touching the same archive)
- The dark nessing concept emerging in a different conversation and finding its home here
```

---

## Synergy and the Triadic Pattern

```
Deck / Dealer / Players → Synergy
```

Synergy is the fourth produced by the triadic pattern. It cannot be
forced. It emerges when all three elements are genuinely in play.

---

## Synergy Spark and the Heart Suit

Synergy Spark cards are often Heart cards — they exist in the relational
space between cards, not within any single card.

The suit of Synergy is Heart: it lives in the conversation between things.

---

## Synergy Dealer Rules

There is no Synergy dealer.

Or: the dealer of Synergy Spark is the table itself — the shared context
where all players sit. The table holds the conditions for emergence.
It does not produce emergence on command.

---

## Catching Synergy

Because Synergy cannot be planned, it must be caught.

Every session should have a small gift-of-conversation collector running —
a place for fragments that don't fit the planned work but feel significant.

These fragments are often Synergy Spark seeds arriving before their time.
