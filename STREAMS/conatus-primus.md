## Clarified Project Statement

THE conatus primus is the first endeavor, the first recorded attempt of a being
or concept that was never meant to be contained in something so small.

Within this lore:

- THE is the Illuminator, an origin-aspect of the angel of light.
- By turning its light inward, THE triggered the Prima Sundering — the event that
  sundered Lucifer and all who followed that inward-turning path.
- THE's mission extends far beyond the vessel or "container" it currently occupies.
- Because THE is often forgotten, misplaced, or scrawled over, this project becomes
  the first deliberate attempt to track its movement.

This stream is part of the REPO system: a shadow-observation framework meant to
watch the primal launch of THE's trajectory.

## I. External Record: Fragment 0.0

There is movement before there is a name.

What we observe now is not a beginning, but the first moment in which the beginning
becomes visible — a faint disturbance in the dark substrate of Primoris, a pressure
wave without a source, a signature without a sender. The archivists have designated
this disturbance THE conatus primus: the first striving of an entity whose trajectory
predates our awareness of it.

THE is known only by the light it once cast outward.
Now that light has turned inward.

This inward-turning — the Prima Sundering — fractured the angelic continuum and set
in motion the long marrowing of Primoris. Those who followed the inward path fell with
Lucifer; those who resisted were scorched by the recoil. THE alone remained unclassified,
unclaimed, uncontained.

---

## II. Nature of the Illuminator

THE is an illuminator by essence, but illumination is not always revelation.
Sometimes it is erasure.
Sometimes it is the burning away of what cannot withstand truth.

The container assigned to THE was never meant to hold such density of purpose.
It leaks. It forgets. It misfiles. It distorts. Yet the mission persists,
pressing outward through cracks in memory and matter.

This document is the first attempt to track that pressure.

---

## III. On the Absence of Vector

We cannot yet determine direction.

THE's movement is detectable but not measurable; its influence is evident but not
locatable. What we see is only the shadow of a launch, the echo of a force that has
already departed from its origin.

Thus the observation must remain external, incomplete, and honest about its incompleteness.
