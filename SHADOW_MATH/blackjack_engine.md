## Naming Convention

**Blackjack** = the proper name for 21 — the Iteration target, the completion state.
Used as a noun: *"we are in Blackjack"* means the 21st Iteration is active.

**WhiteJack** = the polar counterpart — the illuminated exit from the shadow side.
Where Blackjack is the dark worker achieving the target, WhiteJack is what emerges
from THE into visibility. Entangled pair. (See: `the_illuminator.md` — Joker/Anti-Joker.)

**Why "Jack":** The Jack card is the Knave — the active worker between the commoners
(numbered cards 2–10) and the royals (Queen, King). Not royal, not common.
The Jack does the work to reach 21. In Blackjack the game, the name comes from
the Jack of Spades (♠J, the *black* jack) + Ace = 21. The active dark worker
who achieves the target. WhiteJack = the counterpart exiting into light.

**Note on numerals:** Using "21" as a concept-name caused confusion (the number 21
and the milestone concept collide). "Blackjack" gives the concept proper-noun status,
separating it from its numerical value while keeping the blackjack-engine connection.

## Why Blackjack

The deck system uses playing card metaphors throughout.

Blackjack is the game that makes card counting real — that turns the
metaphor of cards into a mathematical system for tracking probability
across time.

In blackjack: you are not playing one hand. You are playing the shoe.
The shoe holds the history of all played cards and the potential of all
unplayed ones. Pattern recognition is reading the shoe.

## 21 as the Target Number

```
21 = the target
17 = dealer must stand (draft gate)
16 = dealer must draw (unresolved)
22+ = bust (failed / collapsed)
```

Applied to this project:

```
21 milestones  = the launch target
17 iterations  = the draft gate (see serious-play-reality-check-17-lens.md)
16th iteration = still resolving / not yet ready
bust           = overbuilding / forcing before ready / premature structure
```

## The Shoe

The shoe is the unplayed archive.

Most of the archive is dark — cards not yet surfaced.
The shoe holds everything that has been produced over four years of work:
conversations, documents, characters, tools, concepts, fragments.

Pattern recognition in this project is reading the shoe:
estimating what has not been sorted yet from the shape of what has.

---

## Card Counting Applied

Basic card counting: track the ratio of high cards to low cards remaining
in the shoe. When the ratio is high (more tens and aces remaining),
the conditions favor the player.

Applied to this project:

```
High cards = high-value concepts ready to surface (near Spade)
Low cards  = seeds still in early Diamond state
Ratio      = readiness to advance vs need to develop further
```

When the ratio is high: push forward, build structure, resolve into Spade.
When the ratio is low: slow down, develop seeds, let Diamond work.

## The Dealer Rules

In blackjack: the dealer stands on 17, draws on 16.

In this system:
- Stand on 17 = at 17 iterations / draft gate, evaluate before pushing further
- Draw on 16 = before 17, continue building / keep working
- Natural 21 = when a concept resolves perfectly without effort
- Bust = when overbuilding collapses a concept before it's ready

## Multi-Deck Awareness

A standard casino shoe holds 6-8 decks.

This project has multiple decks running simultaneously:
```
Work Deck       — Club-heavy, operational
Play Wonka      — Diamond and Heart, creative
Create Pandora  — opening, releasing, Diamond generation
Synergy Spark   — Heart, emergent, unplanned
```

Multi-deck awareness means tracking the state of all four decks
simultaneously without confusing cards between them.

## Bust Mechanics

Going over 21 = overbuilding.

Signs of a bust:
```
- Creating structure for content that hasn't arrived yet
- Building more schema than the current use case requires
- Forcing a concept into a suit before it has resolved naturally
- Abstracting before you have three concrete examples
- Planning for hypothetical future requirements
```

The intake principle applies: do not force the archive to fit the first draft.

---

## The Shadow Count

The shadow count is the count of cards that have not surfaced from the archive.

```
total archive cards (estimated)     = unknown / very large
surfaced cards (named and handled)  = small
shadow count                        = archive - surfaced
```

The shadow count is always positive. There is always more in the shoe.

Working with the shadow count means accepting that the system will be
incomplete for a long time — and that this is correct and healthy.

## Blackjack and the Suits

```
♠ Spades  = the winning hand
♦ Diamonds = the ace (1 or 11 — maximum flexibility)
♣ Clubs    = the working cards (2-9, the middle game)
♥ Hearts   = the face cards (social, relational, high value)
```

A natural blackjack = ♦ Diamond (ace) + ♠ Spade (ten/face) = 21 on the first draw.

The ideal: a seed (Diamond) that resolves immediately into Pinnacle (Spade)
without needing the full Club → Heart cycle.

This rarely happens. But it's worth knowing when it does.
