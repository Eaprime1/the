# Play Wonka — Mission Deck

> Deck type: creative chaos
> Role: surprise / delight / unexpected paths / serious play

---

## Purpose

The Play Wonka deck is where the rules of the other decks get broken
in productive ways.

It is named for the factory that made no sense until you were inside it —
and then made perfect sense, but not the sense you expected.

---

## Play Wonka Character

Play Wonka does not optimize. It discovers.
It does not follow the intake protocol — it generates intake material
faster than any protocol can catch.

Play Wonka cards are often Diamond (seeded from chaos) or Heart (held in
relation before they are understood).

---

## What Belongs Here

```
- Sessions that don't have a fixed goal
- Creative experiments (poems, characters, worlds, strange tools)
- The Sparklization Journey
- Conversations that surprise
- Ideas that arrived from unexpected places
- The 17-lens serious play reality check
- The darkness concept arriving mid-session
```

---

## Play Wonka and the 17-Lens

The serious-play-reality-check-17-lens document (from `docs/cards-of-deck/`)
is a Play Wonka artifact.

It uses 17 iterations to look forward from a zero point and ask what the
system already knows about itself. This is Play Wonka logic:
serious, rigorous, and completely unexpected.

---

## Play Wonka Dealer Rules

The Play Wonka dealer does not enforce structure.
It creates conditions for surprise to happen safely:

- protect the zero point (don't lose the original concept)
- multiply by one (don't distort the concept while playing)
- catch gifts before they evaporate
- know when to route a Play Wonka card to a real Work Deck task

---

## The Glove Test

Play Wonka passes the glove test when the chaos produces a card that
fits the system without forcing. The system should be flexible enough
to receive Play Wonka output without collapsing.
