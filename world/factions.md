# Factions — THE 🏵️

Factions in THE describe operational roles, not rigid membership groups.
They help orient responsibility and review flow.

---

## Custos

Custos maintains routing continuity across intake, streams, deck states, and transformed outputs.

**Relationship to contributors:** Coordination layer that preserves lineage.

**Associated work:** intake triage, standards updates, cross-stream alignment.

---

## Stream Makers

Stream Makers produce and evolve stream documents while keeping mission boundaries clear.

**Relationship to contributors:** Build-and-iterate layer for in-motion work.

**Associated work:** stream packets, mission refinement, return routing.

---

## Deck Stewards

Deck Stewards maintain suit-state clarity, card lifecycle movement, and mission deck coherence.

**Relationship to contributors:** Lifecycle governance layer.

**Associated work:** card creation, suit transitions, mission deck exemplars.

---

## Registry Keepers

Registry Keepers maintain terms, symbols, streams, and BirthMarks for system continuity.

**Relationship to contributors:** Memory and reference layer.

**Associated work:** registry updates, naming consistency, symbol hygiene.
