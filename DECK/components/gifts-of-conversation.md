# Gifts of Conversation — Relational Fragment Component

> Component type: relational
> Role: captures dialogue fragments that carry emotional or meaningful load

---

## What a Gift Is

A gift of conversation is a fragment of dialogue that carries more than
its literal content.

It is a moment in a conversation where something real was said —
where understanding jumped, where a concept crystallized, where a relationship
deepened, where the right word arrived.

These moments are worth holding separately from the raw transcript.

---

## Gifts Are Heart Cards at the Fragment Level

A full conversation can be a Heart card.
A gift is the aethereal fragment within that conversation —
the piece that would survive even if the rest of the conversation
dissolved into the archive.

---

## Gift Format

```yaml
gift:
  id: ""
  timestamp: ""
  from: ""              # who or what said it
  to: ""                # who or what received it
  context: ""           # brief description of the conversation
  content: |
    [the exact words or close paraphrase]
  significance: ""      # why this fragment is held separately
  card: ""              # which card this gift belongs to
  suit_at_time: ""      # what suit the card was in when this gift was given
```

---

## How Gifts Are Used

A gift can be:

- embedded in a card as relational history
- quoted in a liminal README to orient new readers
- used as a sparkle capsule's narrative anchor
- referenced in a quest to show the origin of a choice
- passed between agents as context

---

## The Gift and the Giver

A gift records who gave it — even if the giver is a collaboration tool, a conversation,
a chance observation, or an unknown voice from the archive.

Gifts are not anonymous. They carry their lineage.
