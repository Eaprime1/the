## Why 60

60 is the smallest number divisible by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6.

```
60 ÷ 1 = 60
60 ÷ 2 = 30
60 ÷ 3 = 20
60 ÷ 4 = 15
60 ÷ 5 = 12
60 ÷ 6 = 10
```

This is why humans settled on 60 for time and angle measurement.
It divides cleanly into more shapes than any other small number.

60 is not arbitrary. 60 is the first number where everything fits.

## 60 as the Rotational Backbone

60 degrees is the angle in an equilateral triangle.
Six equilateral triangles tile a hexagon — the honeycomb, the most stable packing.

```
60° × 6 = 360° = full rotation
```

Applied to the deck cycle:

```
each suit occupies 72° of the full 360° circle (360 ÷ 5 = 72)
each card within a suit occupies 6° (72 ÷ 12 = 6)
```

This means every card has a position in the full rotational cycle of the deck.
Card tracking in the sixty matrix is angular positioning — where on the circle
does this card currently sit?
