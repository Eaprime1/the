# Work Deck — Mission Deck

> Deck type: operational
> Role: tasks / builds / deployments / carbonites / active Club work

---

## Purpose

The Work Deck is where things get made.

Every card in the Work Deck is oriented toward a concrete output:
a file, a commit, a tool, a test, a branch, a deployment, a resolved quest.

---

## Work Deck Character

The Work Deck does not play — it builds.
It does not dream — it ships.
It does not hold potential — it turns potential into carbonite.

The Work Deck is the most Club-heavy deck. Its cards are always in motion
or freshly frozen.

---

## Cards That Belong Here

```
- Active branches and PRs
- Tools being built (thee.sh, yod.sh, ember.sh, etc.)
- Quests in progress
- Documents being drafted
- Systems being configured
- Session tasks with concrete completion criteria
```

---

## Work Deck Dealer Rules

The Work Deck dealer:
- tracks what is in flight
- flags when a card has been held in Club too long without progress
- suggests carbonite when a session is ending
- routes completed cards toward Spade evaluation

---

## Work Deck and the Turn Log

Every Work Deck session should produce a turn log entry in `turns/log.md`.

The turn log is the Work Deck's record of what was built and when.

---

## Work Deck Drop States

```
0 = task exists but not started
1 = singular active build underway
5 = multiple parallel builds (e.g. multiple agents working simultaneously)
```
