# Create Pandora — Mission Deck

> Deck type: release / emergence
> Role: open boxes / release potential / unknown outcomes

---

## Purpose

The Create Pandora deck is for moments of opening.

Every card in this deck is a box that has not yet been opened — or one
that is in the act of opening. The outcome is unknown. The act is chosen.

---

## Pandora Character

Pandora does not wait for certainty. It opens.

It is not reckless — it is deliberate about the act of opening while
accepting that what emerges cannot be fully predicted.

Create Pandora cards often generate new Diamond seeds that scatter into
other decks. The opening produces.

---

## What Belongs Here

```
- New repository initializations
- First sessions with a new tool or agent
- The first commit to a new branch
- Publishing a document or system publicly
- Inviting a new participant into the project
- Releasing a branch from protected to open
- The moment a concept leaves private notes and enters shared space
```

---

## Pandora and the Archive

The four-year archive is a Pandora box.

Every document in the archive is a box that hasn't been fully opened
in the context of this system. The Card Intake protocol is the tool
for opening carefully — one card at a time, without forcing.

---

## Pandora Dealer Rules

The Pandora dealer:
- marks each opening with a timestamp (everything released gets a moment)
- creates a Diamond seed for each emergent concept
- does not force the opened content into a final form immediately
- holds what emerges in a hopechest until it can be sorted

---

## Pandora and the Fifth Suit Aperture

When an opening produces a concept that doesn't fit the origin four suits,
it goes to the fifth suit aperture.

Create Pandora is the deck most likely to generate aperture-candidates.
