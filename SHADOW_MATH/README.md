> The mathematical backbone of the deck system.

## What This Folder Is

SHADOW_MATH is the mathematical substrate underneath the card metaphors.

The deck uses playing card language — suits, hands, the shoe, the count —
but those metaphors are not decoration. They map to real mathematical structures.
SHADOW_MATH makes those structures explicit.

## How They Connect

**dark_nessing** is the foundation. Everything else sits on it.
Dark nessing is state 0 in the five-binary system. It is the shadow count in the blackjack engine.
It is the pre-π space before the circle forms.

**blackjack_engine** is the operational layer. It gives the system
its practical vocabulary: the shoe, the count, the 17/21 gate, the bust mechanics.
Every other document in SHADOW_MATH is a layer underneath or alongside the engine.

**five_binary** is the state machine. Five states (0–5) map the full card lifecycle.
Binary underlies everything; the five-prime breaks binary symmetry.
The suits are the five-prime rendered as navigational vocabulary.

**sixty_matrix** is the rotational structure. 60 = 5 × 12.
A complete concept deck is 60 cards. The circle is 360°. Every card has a position.
The sixty matrix gives SHADOW_MATH its clock — its session pacing and cycle tracking.

**polar_trigger_19_23** is the decision system. 19 is the stand-or-draw inflection point.
23 is the confirmed bust. The band 19–23 is where every project decision has maximum consequence.
The polar trigger is the warning system that prevents over-building (bust).

**pi_ln_trig_triad** is the growth and relation model. π closes cycles.
ln describes how the archive reveals itself over time (fast early, slow late).
Trig describes the angular distance between any two suit-states at any moment.

## What SHADOW_MATH Is Not

SHADOW_MATH is not a formal mathematical paper.
It is not a proof system.

It is the mathematical intuition underlying the card metaphors —
made explicit enough to use, without being forced prematurely into rigor.

When the concepts in SHADOW_MATH are ready to be formalized, they will be.
Until then: they are nessing here, doing their work in the dark.
