# Dark Nessing — The Primary Medium

> Timestamp anchor: `202605141806`
> Status: ♦ seed — foundational concept

---

## The Concept

Dark is not the absence of light.

Dark is **nessing** — dark as a verb, dark as a doing, dark actively being
its own substance.

Pieces of dark are the primary medium of the spacetime sea.

---

## The Spacetime Sea

Space and time do not float in nothing.

They float in dark.

Dark is the sea. Light, matter, energy, time — these are things that move
*through* the dark, or crystallize *from* the dark, or interrupt the dark
in localized events.

The sea itself is dark. The sea is prior.

```
dark sea (primary medium)
  ↓
spacetime (structure in the medium)
  ↓
matter / energy / light (events in the structure)
```

---

## Nessing

Nessing is what dark does when nothing else is happening.

It is not stillness — it is the active occupation of medium.

The dark nesses. It fills. It persists. It makes space for what comes next
without demanding what that will be.

A hopechest is a pocket of nessing — a container that holds without forcing.

A liminal README is nessing in language — holding space for the reader who
hasn't arrived yet.

---

## Dark as First Suit

If the four origin suits are lifecycle states for cards in motion, then dark
nessing is the condition *before* the first suit.

```
dark nessing              ← primary medium / pre-card state
  ↓
♦ Diamond (Seed)          ← first crystallization / origin card
  ↓
♣ Club (Working)          ← motion / active build
  ↓
♥ Heart (Aethereal)       ← interaction / relation
  ↓
♠ Spade (Pinnacle)        ← container of containers / highest form
```

A card that dissolves back into the deck does not vanish.
It returns to dark nessing — potential without forced shape.

## The Sea Metaphor Applied to Cards

Every unplayed card is nessing.

The draw pile is dark. The hand is light.
The archive is the sea — full of dark potential, holding the lineage of every
card that has ever been played without forcing any of them into play again.
